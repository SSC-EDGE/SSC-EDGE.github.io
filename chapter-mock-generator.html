Chapter-wise mocks from existing questions

Pick a subject and chapters. For each chapter, its questions (that are not already in a mock of that chapter's Practice Drill folder) are split into sets of about 25 and shown in Practice Drill → Subject → Group → Chapter. A mock always has 20+ questions: chapters with fewer than 20 free questions get no mock. Maths, English and Reasoning mocks go into their own group folder automatically. Nothing is imported again, so no duplicate questions are created.

Fix existing chapter mocks

Scans mocks already inside Practice Drill chapter folders. 1) Time is set to 0.8 min per question (uses the field above) and total questions / section counts are corrected. 2) A mock with fewer than 20 questions is merged into the smallest other mock of the same chapter; the emptied mock is turned inactive (not deleted, so student attempts stay). If a whole chapter has fewer than 20 questions, its mock is turned inactive. Nothing is changed until you press Apply.
