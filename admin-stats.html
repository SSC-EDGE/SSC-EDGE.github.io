How these are counted
• Registered = accounts created on the website (guests are not counted).
• Active = the user opened the website, logged in, or finished a mock in that period.
• Attempted a mock = users with at least one finished mock in that period; Total mock attempts counts every finished attempt.
• Registered but inactive = registered users who were not active in that period (for “All time”: never used the site after registering).
• Website opens are only recorded from the day activity.js is added to your pages; before that, activity is based on login time and mock attempts.
