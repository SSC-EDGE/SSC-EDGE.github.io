📝 Bulk Solutions for Existing Questions

Adds or updates the Explanation and Solution Video Link of questions that are already in your Question Bank. Nothing else about the question changes, and no new questions are created.

Step 1 — Download your questions

Gives an Excel file with every question of the subject (and its current solution). Write the solutions in the Explanation column and save.

Step 2 — Upload the filled sheet

Columns used: Question ID (best — comes from Step 1) or Question English (matched by exact text), plus Explanation and/or Video Link. A Subject column is optional and only narrows text matching. Any other columns are ignored.
